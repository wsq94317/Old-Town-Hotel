Hotel surface art

Walnut, Plaster and Linen are original generated base-color textures produced
with the built-in image_gen tool. Full prompts are in GenerationRecord.json.
The game shader maps them in world space and derives small surface-normal
variations at shading time. No light or shadows are painted into the maps.

Use Old Town Hotel > Art > Polish Surfaces And Interior Lighting to rebuild
the authored scene. Beveled meshes, folded curtains, cloth cushions, local
fixtures, reflection capture and contact shadows are part of this art pass.

Existing gameplay and save files remain authoritative for room state, wear,
construction progress and furniture selection.
